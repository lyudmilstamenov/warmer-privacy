Warmer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

Warmer is an offline word-guessing game for Android developed by Lyudmil Stamenov ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and with whom it is shared.

No accounts, no personal profiles

Warmer does not require an account. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, or location. All gameplay data (guesses, results, achievements, streaks, spark balance) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Third-party services

The app uses the following Google services, which may collect data as described in the Google Privacy Policy:

  • Google AdMob — shows optional rewarded video ads (for example, to earn sparks or unlock practice games). AdMob may process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and coarse ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. In the European Economic Area and the UK you will be asked for consent before any ads are shown, and you can choose non-personalized ads.
  • Google Play Billing — processes optional in-app purchases of sparks. Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment information.
  • Firebase Remote Config — fetches app configuration values (such as prices and feature flags). It uses a randomly generated installation identifier, not tied to your identity.
  • Firebase Analytics — may collect anonymous usage and diagnostic events (such as puzzles played and crashes) to help us improve the game.

Children

Warmer is a general-audience word game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored game data. To request deletion of any data held by the third-party services above, or for any privacy question, contact us at lyudmil.stamenov@gmail.com.
